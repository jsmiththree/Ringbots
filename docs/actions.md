# Actions

Source: the eight action categories. Every action is one of these. A Head or Arm Technique then adds a further effect; those are listed in [techniques.md](techniques.md).

Grapple attacks use Reach and Proximity. Ranged attacks use Range and Remoteness.

The Scope column is the action's width class. It is separate from the derived Scope value (`SCP`) in [resolution.md](resolution.md), which is a numeric tolerance computed from the user's Attribute. The source does not give angles or distances for Standard, Wide, Narrow, or Unlimited.

A Quirk is an extra effect that comes from the action category. A Technique is an extra effect that comes from the specific action.

| Attribute | Description | Scope | Quirk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strike | A quick Grapple attack. | Standard | None. |
| Berserk | A powerful Grapple attack. | Wide | User's Evasion becomes 0. User's Power is boosted relative to the user's Propulsion. |
| Shoot | A quick Ranged attack. | Standard | None. |
| Aim Shot | A powerful Ranged attack. | Narrow | User's Evasion becomes 0. User's Power is boosted relative to the user's Defense. |
| Defend | A defensive action. | Narrow | Increases the Active time of the user's Parry. |
| Heal | A recovery action. | Wide | None. |
| Support | A support action. | Unlimited | None. |
| Interrupt | A utility action. | Standard | None. |

Parry itself is defined with the other mechanical terms in [resolution.md](resolution.md). It answers Strike, Berserk, Shoot, and Aim Shot.

The Berserk and Aim Shot power boosts have no formula. Aim Shot's quirk names Defense, which is not a base stat.
